# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions. Critique behavior and code, not people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats and sharing private information are not welcome.

Keep reports relevant to Funkin Atlas. Do not post unauthorized mod assets, exploit payloads or personal paths in public threads. Maintainers may hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary.

For a sensitive security report, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
